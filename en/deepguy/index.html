The story behind the game

The real submarine

The DeepGuy II in the game really exists. It is an ROV – a tethered underwater filming robot we are building at the studio: seven 3D-printed sections, a 5-megapixel camera in the nose and video straight to a Quest headset. The parts you collect in the game are parts of the real sub.
